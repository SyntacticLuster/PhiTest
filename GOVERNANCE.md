# Governance

## Current status

ɸTest is a single-maintainer project at V1. There is no formal governance body.

## Decision-making

Architectural and scientific changes are reviewed by the maintainer against two criteria:

1. The epistemic boundary defined in `docs/scientific_contract.md` must not be weakened.
2. Changes must be reproducible and falsifiable — no untestable claims about consciousness or qualia may be introduced.

## Contributions

Public contributions are welcome via pull request. Acceptance of a contribution does not imply endorsement of any theory of consciousness.

## Evolution

Governance will be revisited if the project gains multiple active maintainers.
